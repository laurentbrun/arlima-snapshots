# arlima-snapshot

A generated offline snapshot of the ARLIMA website for private, non-commercial research.

This repository contains a research copy of the published website, not its original source code.

## Purpose

The snapshot provides a local copy of ARLIMA pages for offline consultation, searching, and analysis, including with AI-assisted tools running locally.

It supports research into medieval texts, manuscripts, editions, and related scholarship.

## Intended use and retrieval

## AI-assisted analysis

Generated summaries, extracted metadata, and other analytical outputs are research aids, not authoritative reproductions of ARLIMA entries. Check them against the preserved pages and, where appropriate, the current website and cited scholarship.

## Citation

Cite ARLIMA as the source rather than treating this repository as the author of the copied material. Record the relevant entry title, original URL, and consultation date, following the citation style used in your work.

Whenever possible, cite permanent URLs ("permaliens") at the bottom of most pages instead of the URL shown in the browser's adress bar.

When relevant to reproducibility, also identify the snapshot capture date and distinguish it from the date on which the live website was consulted.

Scholarly citation does not replace the attribution and notice requirements of the licence when sharing licensed material or adaptations.
